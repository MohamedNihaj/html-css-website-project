What are your opening hours?

The project concept uses daily opening hours of 12 noon to 10 pm. As this is a student demo website, hours are sample information and would need confirmation before a real visit.

Where are you located?

NHJ Ember is described in this project as a family-run restaurant on Galle Road, Colombo 03, Sri Lanka. The Contact page shows an illustrated map and an optional live map of the general area, but the pin is approximate, not a verified address.

Can I book a table online?

Yes, you can complete the reservation enquiry form on the Contact page. The form checks required fields using built-in browser validation, then opens your email app with the enquiry ready to send. It does not confirm a booking, so please call the restaurant using verified contact details before visiting.

Do you cater for vegetarians?

The sample menu includes vegetarian dishes and vegetarian options. Look for the “V” tag. Please tell the team about any dietary needs when enquiring.

How spicy is the food?

Some dishes carry a spicy tag. Spice levels can vary by dish, so mention your preferred heat level in the special requests box. The kitchen would confirm what adjustments are possible.

Can you accommodate groups and families?

The concept restaurant has approximately 60 seats. For a larger group or a family gathering, use the enquiry form and include the number of guests and any seating needs.

Is parking available?

Parking arrangements have not been verified for this student project. Please contact the venue directly before your visit to ask about current parking options nearby.

How are allergies handled?

Please list allergies in the special requests field and speak with restaurant staff before ordering. A menu tag is not a guarantee against cross-contact in a shared kitchen.
