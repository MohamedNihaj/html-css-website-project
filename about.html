A family story,
told through fire.

We started small, kept our recipes close, and made room for more people to join the table.

Since 2014

Good food is
how we say welcome.

NHJ Ember began as a little charcoal grill on Galle Road. The idea was simple: cook Sri Lankan food with care, let the fire do its work, and treat every guest like a friend.

Today, our 60-seat dining room is still guided by those early habits. Our open-fire cooking style and family recipes remain at the centre of each service. The room has grown, but the spirit has stayed familiar — warm plates, bold flavours and time well spent together.

We sit between the casual comfort of a roadside kade and the polish of a hotel restaurant. Come as you are, bring your people, and let us take care of dinner.

  • Charcoal grilling at the heart of our kitchen
  • Recipes shaped by three generations of family cooking
  • Relationships with Sri Lankan spice growers and day-boat fishers
  • A welcoming menu with clear dietary information

What matters to us

Three simple promises.

Our values are the small decisions we make every day — in the kitchen, with our suppliers and around your table.

01 / PRESERVE

Keep tradition alive.

Protect charcoal cooking and the family recipes that give our dishes their roots.

02 / SOURCE

Support local hands.

Celebrate the growers, fishers and makers who bring the flavours of Sri Lanka to us.

03 / WELCOME

Make room for everyone.

Clear menu notes, warm service and a simple way to enquire about a table.
