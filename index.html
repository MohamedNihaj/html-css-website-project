Our fire, our heritage
Our story

Rooted in the island.
Made around a fire.

What began as a small roadside grill in 2014 has grown into a neighbourhood dining room with the same family spirit.

We believe a meal should feel generous: familiar Sri Lankan flavours, a little charcoal smoke, and enough time to enjoy the people around the table.
